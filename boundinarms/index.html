The Story of How

Bound In Arms

Began

Bound In Arms is Kate Lindley's debut novel—a story she first began writing at fourteen. Over the years, through rewrites and periods spent away from the story, it gradually evolved into the novel it is today.

Returning to a story that never stopped calling her name, Kate discovered that it changed not only on the page but also reshaped her understanding of writing itself—what stories can do and what they can ask of both the writer and the reader.

Genre & Audience

Bound In Arms is a literary novel that blends contemporary romance, consequences of war, dystopian tension, psychological depth, and family saga into one immersive story. It follows characters whose lives are shaped by addiction, abuse, and the lasting effects of survival.

This is a novel for readers who love to connect deeply with characters—who want stories that stay with them long after the final page.

Bound In Arms offers a journey of resilience, redemption, and the unforgettable choices that define us, appealing to anyone drawn to emotionally rich, character-driven fiction.

Unique Storytelling

Bound In Arms is not just a debut novel—it's an immersive journey into lives layered with love, trauma, resilience, and redemption. Spanning generations and intertwining multiple perspectives, it explores how choices, loss, and survival shape people.

Unlike many novels in its genre, Bound In Arms doesn't rely on archetypes or clichés. Brooklyn, Chase, Dallas, Mark, and Austen are fully realized, morally complex characters whose emotional truths feel authentic and earned. Readers experience their triumphs, heartbreaks, and flaws so intimately that they don't just read their story… they live it.
